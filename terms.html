Booking terms

The terms for joining Lady Athena as paying crew. Please read them before you pay a deposit.

Who you book with

Captain Alf Grell, Sövallsvägen 23, 457 95 Grebbestad, Sweden. Phone and WhatsApp +46 793 594 169. Email alf.grell@me.com.

How booking works

  1. You send a booking request for a leg. The request is free and does not commit you.
  2. Alf replies within one day and confirms whether a berth is available.
  3. You pay a 50% deposit. Your berth is secured when the deposit is paid.
  4. You pay the balance no later than 14 days before the start of the leg.

Refunds

Payments are not refunded later than 14 days before the start of the leg, if the voyage is interrupted, or if you leave the yacht early.

What the price covers

Costs are shared between the crew per person per day. The price covers food, diesel, harbour and customs fees, and crew accident insurance. Flights, travel to and from the yacht, your own travel insurance and costs ashore are not included.

Requirements

  • You have some boating experience.
  • You are in good health.
  • You can swim 250 metres.
  • You have your own travel insurance to and from the yacht.

On board

You join as crew, not as a passenger. You take part in watches, steering, sail handling, cooking, cleaning and maintenance. All new crew attend a safety briefing on the first day and follow the captain’s standing orders. Life jackets are worn on deck during passages. Drugs are not allowed on board.

A crew member who ignores the standing orders or behaves offensively may be sent home at their own cost.

Route and weather

The captain decides the route, the stops and the timing based on weather and safety. Harbours and arrival details are sent when you book.
