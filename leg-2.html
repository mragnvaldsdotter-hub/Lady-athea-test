Leg 2 · Isla Mujeres → Jamaica

Christmas at sea, the Cayman walls and Jamaica

Offshore passage

18 Dec 2026 – 2 Jan 2027 · 2–15 days · 550 nautical miles on the full leg

Book this leg All legs

The route

Fifteen days, 550 nautical miles and six nights at sea. You spend Christmas and New Year on board, crossing the Western Caribbean from Mexico to Jamaica. On the way you stop at Little Cayman and Cayman Brac, two small islands with reef walls dropping more than 1,000 metres.

The stops

  1. 1

    Isla Mujeres

    Mexico. Departure. See on Google Maps

  2. 2

    Little Cayman

    Cayman Islands. Fewer than 200 residents and the Bloody Bay Wall, where the reef falls from 6 metres to more than 1,000. See on Google Maps

  3. 3

    Cayman Brac

    Cayman Islands. A limestone bluff rises 43 metres above the sea, with caves to climb into. See on Google Maps

  4. 4

    Montego Bay

    Jamaica. Jerk chicken, reggae and the end of the leg. See on Google Maps

The plan for each day, and exact harbours, are shared when you book and can change with the weather.
