Life aboard

Lady Athena is not a charter. You join as crew, share the work and learn real sailing from the captain and the mate.

The yacht

Lady Athena is a Tayana 58, a fully equipped and comfortable ocean cruiser designed by Pieter Beeldsnijder. She is stable, roomy and well suited to long passages.

On board there is a life raft, life jackets, fire extinguishers, radar, an EPIRB emergency beacon, VHF and SSB radio, an autopilot and Starlink for communication.

Where you sleep

Guests sleep in a cabin with a real bed, standing headroom and thick mattresses, not in a berth in the saloon. There is a guest toilet and shower on board.

  • Port cabin: three berths (one double, one single).
  • Starboard cabin: two single berths.
  • Maximum five guests. The aft cabin is the captain’s own and is not part of the guest accommodation.

A day aboard

Everyone shares the work, guided by the captain and the mate: sail handling, steering, navigating, cooking and washing up, cleaning and maintenance.

On longer passages the crew is divided into watches, and at night at least two crew members are awake on deck. Close to land you can go ashore, snorkel, kayak or cycle.

One hot meal is served each day. You can make your own breakfast, sandwiches and snacks at any time.

Safety

Safety comes first. Captain Alf follows the weather closely, plans the route and adjusts the sailing to the conditions.

  • A safety briefing for all new crew on the first day
  • Life jackets are worn on deck during passages
  • Ask the captain whenever you are unsure

We cannot promise that nothing will ever go wrong at sea. We promise to take safety seriously and to explain how everything works before you need it.

What to bring

  • Light rain gear
  • Sunglasses and a hat
  • Sun protection
  • A soft bag, no larger than a cabin bag

Who it suits

  • You are healthy and can swim 250 metres.
  • You have your own travel insurance to and from the yacht.
  • You have some boating experience. Experience of long ocean passages is not required.
  • You enjoy teamwork. Tell us your experience when you send your request, and Alf will advise on the best leg for you.

House rules

  • No drugs.
  • No eating or drinking in your berth. Eat in the saloon, galley or on deck.
  • Keep your belongings in a soft bag in your cabin, not in the saloon.
  • Leave your cabin as you found it, and everyone helps clean the yacht before leaving.
  • If the standing orders are ignored or someone behaves offensively, they may be sent home at their own cost.
