Choose your leg

Ocean passages include night sailing and suit people who are comfortable at sea. Island-hopping legs are day sailing only. Dates are flexible: you can join or leave at ports where you can clear in and out.

Leg 1 · Rio Dulce → Isla Mujeres

From a jungle river to Mexico, across 600 miles of reef

Offshore passage

1–14 Dec 2026
2–14 days · 600 nautical miles on the full leg

You leave the Rio Dulce, a river lined with rainforest, and sail 600 nautical miles north to Isla Mujeres in Mexico. You spend four days and nights under way and learn to stand watch in the dark. Between passages you anchor off the Bay Islands of Honduras and Lighthouse Reef in Belize, both on the Mesoamerican Barrier Reef, the second largest barrier reef on the planet.

Harbours and arrival details are sent when you book.

Leg 2 · Isla Mujeres → Jamaica

Christmas at sea, the Cayman walls and Jamaica

Offshore passage

18 Dec 2026 – 2 Jan 2027
2–15 days · 550 nautical miles on the full leg

Fifteen days, 550 nautical miles and six nights at sea. You spend Christmas and New Year on board, crossing the Western Caribbean from Mexico to Jamaica. On the way you stop at Little Cayman and Cayman Brac, two small islands with reef walls dropping more than 1,000 metres.

Leg 3 · Jamaica → British Virgin Islands

760 miles of blue water to the Virgin Islands

Offshore passage

9–23 Jan 2027
2–14 days · 750–770 nautical miles on the full leg

The longest crossing of the season, with seven nights at sea. If you want offshore experience before a bigger voyage of your own, this is your leg. You go ashore in the Dominican Republic between passages and finish in the British Virgin Islands.

Short sails, flamingos and white sand

Island hopping

1–14 Feb 2027
2–14 days · 137 nautical miles on the full leg

Day sailing only. You cover 137 nautical miles in 14 days, about 10 a day, so most of your time goes to swimming, snorkelling and going ashore. A good first leg if you have some boating experience but have never sailed offshore.

Leg 5 · St Martin → Antigua

Five islands, four flags, two World Heritage sites

Island hopping

20 Feb – 6 Mar 2027
2–14 days · 230 nautical miles on the full leg

Day sailing only, 230 nautical miles over two weeks. The language and the history change with every stop: French St Barth, Dutch Statia, and British forts and dockyards on St Kitts and Antigua. Plan on long lunches ashore.

Twenty days of volcanoes, rainforest and French islands

Island hopping

The longest island-hopping leg: 270 nautical miles over 20 days, so you spend more time ashore than at sea. Walk the ruins of Saint-Pierre, take a rowing boat up the Indian River on Dominica and swim in the bay at Les Saintes.

Leg 7 · Martinique → Grenada

Turtles, turquoise water and the Grenadines

Island hopping

11–25 Apr 2027
2–14 days · 175 nautical miles on the full leg

The last leg of the season. 175 nautical miles of short hops through St Lucia, St Vincent and the Grenadines, with time to swim at most stops. Snorkel in the Tobago Cays, where green turtles feed in the sea grass, and finish in Grenada, where nutmeg grows on the hillsides.

How booking works

  1. Choose a leg and send your booking request. It costs nothing and does not commit you.
  2. Alf replies within one day to confirm availability and answer your questions.
  3. You pay a 50% deposit to secure your place.
  4. The balance is due at least 14 days before the start. Harbours and arrival details are sent when you book.

Refunds are not given later than 14 days before the start, or if a voyage is interrupted.

What the price covers

Costs are shared between the crew per person per day. They cover food, diesel, harbour and customs fees, and crew accident insurance. Flights and your own travel insurance are not included.
