Frequently asked questions

How does booking work?

Choose a leg and send your booking request. It costs nothing and does not commit you. Alf replies within one day to confirm availability. You pay a 50% deposit to secure your place, and the balance is due at least 14 days before the start.

Is the price per person?

Costs are shared between the crew per person per day. Ask for the current price for your leg when you send your request.

What is included?

Food, diesel, harbour and customs fees, and crew accident insurance. Flights, your own travel insurance and costs ashore are not included.

Can I cancel?

Refunds are not given later than 14 days before the start, or if a voyage is interrupted or you leave the yacht early. Read the full booking terms before you pay.

Do I need sailing experience?

Yes. You need some boating experience and should feel comfortable at sea. You do not need experience of long ocean passages. Tell us what you have done when you send your request, and Alf will advise on the best leg for you.

What are the requirements?

You should be healthy and able to swim 250 metres. You need your own travel insurance to and from the yacht.

Where do I sleep?

In a guest cabin with a real bed. The port cabin has three berths (one double, one single) and the starboard cabin has two single berths. There is a maximum of five guests on each leg.

Is this a charter?

No. You join as crew and share watches, steering, sail handling and galley duties with the captain and the mate. You learn by taking part.

Can I join for part of a leg?

Dates are flexible. You can join or leave at ports where you can clear in and out. Write in your booking request which dates you would like, and Alf will tell you what is possible and what it costs.

Where does the leg start and finish?

Harbours and arrival details are sent when you book.

Is it safe?

Safety comes first. Alf follows the weather closely, holds a safety briefing on day one, and life jackets are worn on deck during passages. Sailing always carries some risk, and we cannot guarantee that nothing will go wrong. We take safety seriously and explain how everything works before you need it.
