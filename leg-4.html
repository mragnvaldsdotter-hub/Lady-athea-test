Leg 4 · British Virgin Islands → St Martin

Short sails, flamingos and white sand

Island hopping

1–14 Feb 2027 · 2–14 days · 137 nautical miles on the full leg

Book this leg All legs

The route

Day sailing only. You cover 137 nautical miles in 14 days, about 10 a day, so most of your time goes to swimming, snorkelling and going ashore. A good first leg if you have some boating experience but have never sailed offshore.

The stops

  1. 1

    Road Town

    Tortola, British Virgin Islands. Departure. See on Google Maps

  2. 2

    Virgin Gorda

    British Virgin Islands. The Baths, a beach of giant granite boulders with pools and passages between them. See on Google Maps

  3. 3

    Anegada

    British Virgin Islands. The only coral island among the main Virgin Islands. Flat, 18 kilometres long, with flamingos in the salt ponds and lobster on the grill. See on Google Maps

  4. 4

    Anguilla

    Anguilla. 33 beaches on an island 26 kilometres long. See on Google Maps

  5. 5

    Prickly Pear Cays

    Anguilla. Two uninhabited cays with snorkelling straight off the beach. See on Google Maps

  6. 6

    Marigot

    St Martin. The French side of an island shared with the Netherlands since 1648. See on Google Maps

The plan for each day, and exact harbours, are shared when you book and can change with the weather.
