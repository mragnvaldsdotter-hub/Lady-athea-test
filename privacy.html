Privacy notice

How Lady Athena Sailing handles the personal details you send.

Who is responsible

Captain Alf Grell, Sövallsvägen 23, 457 95 Grebbestad, Sweden. Email alf.grell@me.com.

What we collect

When you send a booking request, we receive what you wrote in the form: your name, email address, the leg you are interested in, the dates, the number of people, your sailing experience and your questions.

The form is handled by our website host, Netlify, Inc., which stores the request and forwards it to Alf by email. Netlify is based in the United States. You can read Netlify’s own privacy policy on netlify.com.

Why we use your details

To answer your request, confirm a berth, plan your voyage and keep the records needed for payments and accounting.

How long we keep your details

We keep your messages as long as needed for your voyage and for accounting rules.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Other services

The route maps on the voyage pages load a map library from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and map images from OpenStreetMap. When you open a voyage page, these services receive your IP address so they can send the map to your browser. Links to Google Maps open Google’s website only when you click them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you. Email alf.grell@me.com. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), imy.se.
